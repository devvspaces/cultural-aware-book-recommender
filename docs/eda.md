| Dimension | Code | Description | Missing Values |
|:----------|:-----|:------------|:---------------|

## 4. Cultural Coverage Analysis

### 4a. Hofstede Dimension Correlation Heatmap

**Chart:** `project/eda/4a_hofstede_correlation_heatmap.png`

![Hofstede correlation heatmap](file:///Users/ayomideayanwola/projects/recommenders/movielens/project/eda/4a_hofstede_correlation_heatmap.png)

**What this shows:** Pairwise Pearson correlations between the 6 Hofstede cultural dimensions across all 119 countries.

**Key findings:**
- **PDI and IDV are strongly negatively correlated (r ≈ −0.65)**: Countries with high power distance tend to be collectivist, and vice versa. This is one of Hofstede's most well-documented cultural patterns.
- **IDV and IVR are positively correlated**: Individualistic countries tend to score higher on indulgence.
- **LTO shows moderate negative correlation with IVR**: Societies with long-term orientation tend to be more restrained.
- **MAS is relatively independent** of other dimensions, showing weak correlations — meaning masculinity/femininity captures a distinct cultural axis.
- These correlation patterns validate that all 6 dimensions carry unique information and justify using the full 6-dimensional Hofstede vector in our FM rather than dimensionality-reducing.

### 4b. Power Distance (PDI) vs Individualism (IDV) Cultural Map

**Chart:** `project/eda/4b_pdi_vs_idv_scatter.png`

![PDI vs IDV scatter](file:///Users/ayomideayanwola/projects/recommenders/movielens/project/eda/4b_pdi_vs_idv_scatter.png)

**What this shows:** Each dot is a country plotted by its IDV (x-axis) and PDI (y-axis) scores, colored by UAI. Notable countries are labeled.

**Key findings:**
- **Clear cultural clusters emerge:**

- **African and Asian countries** cluster in the high-PDI, low-IDV quadrant — meaning our culturally aware FM should recommend different types of books to users from these regions compared to Western users.
- The color gradient (UAI) shows that countries like Japan and Russia combine high uncertainty avoidance with moderate power distance — these users may prefer structured, well-established literary genres (classics, historical non-fiction).

### 5a. Long-Tail Popularity Distribution

**Chart:** `project/eda/5a_longtail_popularity.png`

![Long-tail popularity](file:///Users/ayomideayanwola/projects/recommenders/movielens/project/eda/5a_longtail_popularity.png)

**What this shows:** Books ranked by number of ratings (log-log scale), revealing the classic long-tail distribution.

**Key findings:**
- The distribution follows a **near-perfect power law**: a handful of books attract hundreds of thousands of ratings, while the vast majority receive fewer than 10.
- The red threshold line at 5 ratings shows that more than half of all books fall below this minimum — these are essentially invisible to pure collaborative filtering models.
- **This is the core motivation for our culturally aware approach:** Pure collaborative methods (SVD++) cannot recommend books with too few ratings. Our FM v2 can leverage the book's cultural profile (country, language → Hofstede vector) to recommend niche, culturally relevant books even when they lack rating history.

## Summary of Key EDA Insights

| Finding | Implication for Our System |
|:--------|:--------------------------|
| **99.99% sparsity** | Pure collaborative filtering alone cannot cover the matrix. Side information (cultural features) is essential. |
| **Positive rating skew (3–5 stars)** | Models must learn subtle differences within a narrow rating band. |
| **Median 4 ratings/book** | Most books are invisible to CF — cultural and content-based features are needed to surface them. |
| **44.9% missing language codes** | Cultural inference must rely primarily on `country_code` (0.02% missing), with language as fallback. |
| **US-dominated country codes** | Cultural distance features will primarily differentiate non-US users from the US-centric catalog. |
| **PDI ↔ IDV strong negative correlation** | Validates Hofstede theory; both dimensions carry unique but related cultural signals. |
| **7.1% true cold-start users** | Justifies our switching hybrid: FM v2 for cold-start, weighted blend for active users. |
| **Power-law book popularity** | Motivates catalog coverage and novelty metrics alongside precision/recall. |
